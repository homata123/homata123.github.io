The news I follow, gathered and published without a newsroom.

Ada’s Feed is a personal news aggregation and publishing system I built and operate. It automatically collects, curates, and updates content every three hours on AI & technology, cybersecurity, science, games, and video from multiple sources across the internet and Facebook groups, alongside a residents’ bulletin with built-in filtering of personal contact information to protect privacy.

The live feed sits behind sign-in, restricted to a small allowlist. This page is the public record of the project: what it does, how an edition is made, and what runs underneath.

How an edition is made

Four steps, repeated around the clock. No step needs me in the room; I step in only when a source changes or a rule needs adjusting.

1

Collect

Scheduled agents sweep the web and a set of Facebook groups every three hours, in rotating editions: a full morning briefing, then focused updates on AI, science, games, and video through the day, and an evening wrap-up.

2

Curate

Every item must carry a real source link gathered during the run, or it is dropped. Duplicates are filtered against the day’s log. Residents’ posts are scrubbed of phone numbers, personal names, and unit numbers before anything is written down.

3

Publish

New items are committed to a Git repository as a single data file. That one push deploys the site twice: to GitHub Pages and to Cloudflare Pages. There is no database and no server to patch.

4

Read, and ask for more

A refresh button on the site requests a fresh sweep of one topic or of everything. A search box queries the residents’ group directly. Requests travel over a WebSocket relay and results are usually back in about two minutes.

What it covers

The beats were chosen the way a reader chooses them, not the way a publisher would: narrow, personal, and revised when my interests move.

AI, technology and security

Model releases, agent tooling, and the serious end of the security beat: actively exploited vulnerabilities, major breaches, and official warnings.

Science

Theoretical physics, cosmology, quantum mechanics, mathematics, and astronomy, including new videos from a Vietnamese astronomy channel I follow.

Games and video

AAA games, story and lore, plus new uploads from a short list of Vietnamese YouTube channels. A separate section tracks two favourite channels and embeds their live streams so they can be watched in place.

Facebook groups digest

A tech community’s morning news post leads each edition, joined by alumni, entertainment, and AI-art groups, filtered down to posts with real information in them.

Residents’ bulletin

Food and drink sold inside my own residential complex, building notices, and everyday resident life, collected several times each evening with contact details stripped out.

Under the hood

Front end
One static page in hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework, no build step.
Hosting and access
Cloudflare Pages behind Cloudflare Access: a one-time PIN sign-in checked against a five-email allowlist.
On-demand requests
Pages Functions write requests to Cloudflare KV; a Durable Object hub relays them over WebSocket to the collecting agent, with the KV mailbox as fallback.
Publishing pipeline
Git is the CMS. Each edition is a commit; the site is a data file rendered in the browser.
Collection
Scheduled AI agents with written editorial rules: source link or drop, deduplicate against daily logs, scrub private details.
